# Barcelona Pocket Gamer Banner Spec

## Goal
Create a review-ready conference banner concept for `Pocket Gamer Connects Barcelona`, `June 15-16, 2026`, that stops foot traffic, communicates BOG's real product identity in under 5 seconds, and gives visitors two clean QR destinations:
- TestFlight
- website

## Player Value
For a passerby, the banner should answer three questions immediately:
- What is BOG
- Why is it different
- What should I scan first

The banner should sell the real fantasy already supported by the docs:
- you are a Resolver
- conflicts are settled through AR figurine duels
- the game's strategic signature is lineup building under a fixed `12 danger` budget

## Trigger / Entry Point
Use this banner:
- beside the elephant piece at the BOG conference setup
- in a high-footfall B2B event environment where attendees glance first and only then decide whether to stop
- as a bridge between booth curiosity and direct TestFlight / site conversion

## UX Flow
1. Attendee notices the BOG logo and bold headline from distance.
2. Attendee understands the core fantasy from one short line:
   `Resolve the contract.`
3. Attendee gets the differentiator from the support line:
   `Build the lineup. Lock 12 danger. Watch the duel.`
4. Attendee sees one hero visual zone that implies the BOG world:
   AI terminal, AR protocol, tactile sci-fi interface.
5. Attendee chooses one of two QR paths:
   `Play TestFlight` or `Visit Website`

## Rules
- Do not over-explain systems on the banner.
- Lead with fantasy plus one mechanical differentiator.
- Keep readable from `2-4` meters away.
- Preserve the existing BOG visual language already seen in current materials:
  - pale paper background
  - dark navy interface frames
  - aqua orbit accent
  - orange interaction highlight
- Leave generous quiet space around both QR codes so they can be replaced safely at print stage.
- Keep the composition centered enough that the banner still works if the elephant ends up on either side.

## Rewards / Economy Impact
- Primary reward is booth conversion:
  - scan to TestFlight
  - scan to website
- Secondary reward is sharper positioning in investor and partner conversations because the banner reinforces the actual BOG promise instead of generic sci-fi branding.

## Risks
- If the copy leans too hard into lore, the game type becomes unclear.
- If the copy leans too hard into systems, the banner starts reading like design documentation.
- If the hero zone is too dark or too busy, QR discovery will drop.
- If the final printed QR codes are placed too low, the stand base may make scanning awkward.

## Dependencies
- final TestFlight URL
- final website URL
- approved BOG logo lockup
- one approved hero render or cropped UI/character composition for final production pass
- printer safe-area / bleed specification for the chosen standee vendor

## Open Questions
- Will the banner stand to the left or right of the elephant on-site
- Do we want the QR labels in English only or bilingual
- Which hero asset should lead the final print:
  - AI terminal
  - Resolver character
  - lineup / start-button composition
- Do we want the website QR to go to the public site or directly to the playtest landing page

## Implementation Notes
### Recommended format
- Standee size: `800mm x 2000mm`
- Safe reading zones:
  - top `25%`: brand + hook
  - middle `45%`: hero visual
  - bottom `30%`: conversion zone with two QR codes

### Recommended headline stack
- Primary:
  - `RESOLVE THE CONTRACT.`
- Secondary:
  - `BUILD THE LINEUP. LOCK 12 DANGER. WATCH THE DUEL.`

### Recommended support chips
- `AR FIGURINE DUELS`
- `MOBILE SCI-FI STRATEGY`
- `RESOLVER PROTOCOL`

### Layout direction
- Use a light background so the stand reads clearly in a crowded hall.
- Keep one dominant dark protocol frame in the center to anchor the eye.
- Use the aqua BOG orbit behind the logo and again as a soft motion shape behind the hero zone.
- Reuse the orange `Start` button language as the strongest accent near the lower-middle area.
- Keep QR blocks white and simple so they feel operational, not decorative.

### Recommended QR labels
- Left:
  - `PLAY THE BUILD`
  - `TESTFLIGHT`
- Right:
  - `SEE THE WORLD`
  - `BOG WEBSITE`

### Production note
- If final booth placement is confirmed and the elephant sits on one side, make a mirrored export so the banner's inward-facing edge points attention back toward the elephant and booth center.

## QA / Playtest Checks
- Print the concept at small scale and confirm headline readability first.
- Test both QR codes from a phone camera at roughly `1-1.5` meters.
- Confirm the stand base does not cover the QR quiet zone.
- Confirm the copy matches the actual playable promise:
  - Resolver fantasy
  - AR figurine duels
  - `12 danger` constrained lineup strategy
- Confirm no line implies features the current build cannot emotionally support.
