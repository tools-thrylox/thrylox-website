Choosing the first game

Market and niche research, genre and visual direction, mechanics and balance shaped BOG before production. The result was a defined first project, with its design documented.

Thrylox was incorporated, team contracts arranged, and banking and accounting put in place. The operating foundations were ready for production.

Early external testing

BOG in Barcelona

BOG was presented in Barcelona as part of its early external testing.

We are here

Building the wider game

Production now expands the demo with more locations, figures, quests and PvE content, alongside onboarding, progression and monetisation.

User-submitted cases, the store, battle pass, live operations and ship customisation are planned parts of that work.

Planned

Beta & creative testing

Test the player experience and the stability of the build. In parallel, develop and test advertising creatives to learn how to introduce BOG to new players.

This stage prepares the game for soft launch, where retention and acquisition economics can be tested together.

Planned · launch targets

Soft launch

Soft launch is where the team tests whether players return and whether acquisition and monetisation can support growth. The targets below guide the decision to move towards global launch.

Paid cost per install
≤ $6
Day 1 retention
≥ 35%
Day 30 retention
6–8%
Day 30 return on ad spend
60–70%

Soft-launch targets for progressing towards global launch—not current performance.

When launch targets are met

Global launch. Then grow.

Global launch follows the soft-launch targets. If they are not met, the plan is to keep improving the game before scaling acquisition.

After launch, work continues through new content, live operations and continued improvements to the player experience.
